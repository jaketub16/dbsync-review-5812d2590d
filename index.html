03 · What we got wrong

Our mistakes, stated plainly

1

The opt-in page underperformed

9,877 people clicked, but only 0.64% opted in. The ads did their job and our page and pop-up didn't. That's Aletto's to fix.

2

Targeting was too broad

Only 11 of 63 opt-ins checked out as a real ICP fit. We should have narrowed to data engineers and architects sooner. We agreed to that on Oct 23.

3

Launch was slow

Payment to launch took about 7 weeks, which ate into the momentum you paid for.

4

We didn't monitor follow-up

We never set up call logging in our CRM, and from Sep 14 to late October we didn't ask for updated call reports. Because of that we drew conclusions from a partial picture. We should have closed that gap in week 3, not week 10.

Correction: our earlier page said "46 of 63 leads were never called." That count was wrong, and we own it. It was based on John's Sep 14 export, and 38 of the 63 leads hadn't opted in yet by Sep 14. Of the 25 who had, 21 appear in that export. Across the full report, 4 of our 63 opt-ins show no call. This correction is about that count only: speed to lead and call quality were still real problems (section 04).

What this tells us

The report shows a lot of dialing. It doesn't show fast or productive follow-up. Only 2 of 59 leads got a first call within the 5 minutes we agreed, the typical first call came about 11.7 hours after opt-in, and only 39 of 798 calls lasted a minute or more. Lead quality and targeting are part of this, and we own those above. Speed to lead and call quality are the follow-up side, and both need to change.

None of these calls were logged in the shared GHL CRM, so neither side could see this in real time. Fixing that comes first.
